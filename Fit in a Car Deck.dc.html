What should we measure?
Three candidate questions, each with a reason.
Is it heavier than 150 kg?
Matthew is in the car too: the car has a limit.
Is it longer than 1.5 m?
It has to fit across the back seat.
Is it taller than the car door?
It has to get in.
All three sound reasonable. Only counting mistakes will tell us which one actually works best.

The rejected question

Won't fit, too heavy, not allowed: the same three things you checked in your school bag at the start.
The real algorithms
ID3 and C4.5 score questions using information gain; CART, the one inside most software today, uses Gini impurity. We counted mistakes, which is simpler and gives the same tree here.
